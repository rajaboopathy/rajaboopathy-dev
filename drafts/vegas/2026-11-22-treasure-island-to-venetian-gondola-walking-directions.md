# Treasure Island to Venetian Gondola — Walking Directions

**Parking:** Treasure Island garage is free (self-park and valet), 24 hours. Entrance off Mel Tormé Way. Park on level 3 if possible — that's where the covered pedestrian bridge starts.

**Step 1:** From the garage, take the escalator or stairs up to level 3 and cross the covered pedestrian bridge into Treasure Island casino.

**Step 2:** Walk through the casino toward the east side, following signs for the Venetian bridge.

**Step 3:** Cross the open-air skybridge over Las Vegas Boulevard — about a 5-minute walk, with escalators at both ends.

**Step 4:** Once on the Venetian side, enter the Grand Canal Shoppes and go up to level 2.

**Step 5:** The gondola loading dock is at Emporio d'Gondola.

**Notes:**
- Total walk is about 5 minutes from TI to the Venetian side.
- Right now only the indoor canal route is running; the outdoor lagoon by the Doge's Palace is closed for the season.
- Tickets: $39 per person, or $34 on select November and December dates. About 10–13 minutes per ride.
- The Venetian charges $20–25/day for its own parking, so parking at TI and walking over saves that fee.
